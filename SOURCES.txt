Build: send the three formatted files to their LMX/ names (LISACom itself
is the natural carrier: Receive via YMODEM, type LMX/MAIN.TEXT at Save as),
Save & Put Away the running tool, empty the Wastebasket, then from the
Workshop: R <LMX/MAKE.  Afterwards L LMX/= must show a NEW LMX.OBJ -- the
exec does not stop on a link error.  README.md, Building from source, has
the full list of things that bite.

Hard-won notes living in the comments: the serial typeahead buffer is
pinned at 1024 -- larger kills receive on a Lisa 2/10; the terminal is
model-first (drain the port, paint once) so BBS menu bursts cannot outrun
a 5 MHz machine; Lisa Pascal cannot index a string constant (see cpMap ->
cpTab); '-' is not legal in a catalog name; a VAR parameter must be
declared with the caller's exact named type (Str255 and Pathname are not
interchangeable); global data is capped at 32K by the linker and this
program is right under it; a device with no volume still answers LOOKUP
(check fsi.mounted); closing the serial port does not hang up a WiFi modem
(+++ / ATH with guard times does).

README.md is the full manual; CHANGELOG.md lists 1.2 - 1.7.
